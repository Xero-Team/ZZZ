# Privacy and Security

## Philosophy

ZZZ collects minimal data necessary to serve and improve our product. Features that could share data, like AI requests, are either opt-in or can be disabled.

- **AI**: Data sharing for AI improvement is opt-in, and each share is a one-time action; it does not grant permission for future data collection. You can use ZZZ's AI features without sharing any data with ZZZ and without authenticating.

- **Open-Source**: ZZZ's codebase is public. You can inspect exactly what data is collected and how it's handled. If you find issues, we encourage you to report them.

- **Secure-by-default**: Designing ZZZ and our Service with "secure-by-default" as an objective is of utmost importance to us. We take your security and ours very seriously and strive to follow industry best-practice in order to uphold that principle.
